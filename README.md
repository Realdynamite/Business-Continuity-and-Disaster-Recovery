GRC205 – Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Assignment

Overview

This repository contains my GRC205 – Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery assignment. The project focuses on business continuity planning, disaster recovery strategies, backup and restoration procedures, recovery objectives, incident coordination, and organisational resilience.

The assignment uses a simulated disruption scenario involving Northbridge Skills Institute to assess how an organisation can maintain critical operations, recover essential services, and minimise the impact of unexpected disruptions.

Objectives

Identify critical business functions and assess the impact of service disruptions.

Define Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs), Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs), and Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD).

Evaluate backup strategies and disaster recovery options.

Develop a structured recovery plan with defined roles and responsibilities.

Establish incident communication and escalation procedures.

Conduct a tabletop exercise to evaluate recovery readiness.

Recommend governance, testing, monitoring, and continuous improvement measures.

Scope of the Assignment

The assignment covers six main areas:

Business impact analysis and identification of critical functions.

Recovery objectives, backup assessment, and recovery strategy selection.

Disaster recovery planning and recovery scheduling.

Incident coordination, communication, and operational continuity.

Tabletop exercise, scenario evaluation, and corrective actions.

Governance, testing, performance monitoring, and continuous improvement.

Frameworks and References

The assignment draws on relevant business continuity and disaster recovery guidance, including:

NIST Special Publication 800-34 Revision 1 – Contingency Planning Guide for Federal Information Systems.

ISO 22301:2019 – Business Continuity Management Systems.

Relevant course materials and guidance from the International Cybersecurity Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA).

Scenario

The project examines a simulated flooding and power failure incident affecting Northbridge Skills Institute. It considers the resulting disruption to teaching and assessments, finance, admissions, payroll, and records management, and proposes recovery measures to support the restoration of critical services.
